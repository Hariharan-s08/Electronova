Guidelines

Please follow these guidelines throughout the event.

01

Bring Your Own Connectivity

Bring your laptop and internet dongle. The institute will provide Wi-Fi access. Standalone digital cameras are not permitted.

02

College ID is Mandatory

Carry your college ID as valid proof of identity. No substitute will be accepted.

03

Dress Code

Business casuals are required throughout the full day of the event.

04

Follow Mentor's Direction

Follow the instructions and guidance provided by your assigned mentor.

05

Stay Within Bounds

Remain within the permitted areas. Certain EGSPEC areas are restricted.

06

Keep Your Bench Tidy

Keep your workspace clean and use the designated bins for waste disposal.

07

Photos and Videos

Capture photos or videos only of your project. Phone captures of your own project work are allowed for jury presentation.

08

Mind Your Belongings

Take care of your personal belongings. EGSPEC is not responsible for unattended items.

09

Respect the Campus

Maintain discipline and respect staff, mentors, participants and the campus environment.

10

Restricted Areas

Do not enter classrooms, laboratories, offices or other restricted areas without authorization.

11

Use Equipment Responsibly

Handle all institute and event equipment carefully and responsibly.

12

Maintain Appropriate Noise Levels

Keep noise under control and avoid disturbing other teams, participants or ongoing activities.

13

Be Punctual

Report to the venue on time and follow the announced event schedule.

14

Do Not Disrupt Others

Avoid activities that interfere with other teams, participants or event proceedings.

15

Follow Safety Rules

Follow all safety instructions and immediately report damage, accidents, hazards or emergencies.

16

Keep the Campus Clean

Do not litter or damage campus property. Dispose of waste and food responsibly.

17

Follow the Code of Conduct

Maintain professional behavior, respect everyone, and follow all event rules and instructions throughout the program.

Important: All participants are expected to follow the instructions given by organizers, mentors, jury members, volunteers and institute staff.
